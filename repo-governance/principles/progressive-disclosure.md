# Progressive Disclosure

Present the minimum information needed at the current scope, then reveal supporting detail through clear, intentional links.

## Requirements

- Put a concise, actionable instruction where a person or agent first encounters the task.
- Store rationale, exceptions, examples, and procedures in the most specific authoritative governance document.
- Link to canonical detail instead of copying it into multiple files.
- Give readers enough context at each level to understand the instruction and decide whether to follow a link.
- Never hide a prerequisite, safety boundary, or other non-negotiable constraint behind an optional path.
- Refactor and redistribute content when an entry point becomes crowded instead of allowing it to grow without structure.

Progressive disclosure keeps high-visibility instruction files useful within their word limits, reduces duplicated rules, and lets detailed governance evolve in one canonical place.

This principle is subordinate to the repository [vision](../vision/README.md). All conventions, development standards, and workflows must conform to it.
